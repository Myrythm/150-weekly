## A. #6 Age first, then the age group's WHO 2020 guideline (Opus)

**What changed**
- One central table `GUIDE` (keys `child`, `adult`, `older`) now drives every target and every piece of target copy. The old `TARGET` const is gone. Do not reintroduce a hard-coded 150.
- The visitor's age is stored as `prefs.age` in `w150.prefs` (whole years 0 to 120, or `null`). Old prefs without `age` load fine. They become `unset`, and the age step appears. Clear all data removes the age too, and the confirm copy now says so.
- First visit, or any visitor without a stored age: the age step `#age-step` replaces the log panel `#log` in the hero. On narrow screens it moves right under the headline (`.band-grid.is-asking` swaps grid areas). The sample bib, lane and data stay visible behind it as an adult preview, with a dashed chip that reads "Preview: adult targets until you set your age".
- After an age is set, the chip `#age-chip` shows the group (for example "Adult 18 to 64 guideline"), and `#age-change` reopens the step. When the step is reopened, `#age-cancel` ("Keep my current age") closes it again.
- Validation: empty input, anything that is not a whole number, or an age over 120 shows the inline error "Enter an age from 5 to 120." Ages 0 to 4 are stored and give the under-5 state. In that state the targets block `#targets` and the streak tiles, message and flags are hidden. The h1 and lead change, and the step stays open with `#age-under5` (a kind message and a link to the WHO under-5 guide: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241550536).
- Child group (5 to 17): the target is 420. Vigorous minutes count 1:1. The bib shows "TARGET 420" and the lane runs 0 to 420 (labels every 60). There are 3 strength boxes, plus a new "Vigorous days: N of 3" row `#vig-days`. The next-step line has no running equivalent, and the log helper `#log-int-note` and the success message have no x2.
- Older group (65+): the same aerobic and strength targets as adults. The lead, strength help, footer and FACT_1 mention balance on 3+ days. `balanceDays: 3` is in its config. The tracker itself is fix D.
- Now guide-driven: the bib target `#bib-target`, the lane scale (`buildLane(g)` rebuilds ticks and labels), the meter aria, the legend (the x2 text only shows when the multiplier is above 1), the next step (`nextStepLine(c)`), the strength label, count, boxes and help, the last-week line, the 12-week flags, the heat levels (scaled to the target), the grid aria, the "Weeks at N" tile `#tile-weeks-label`, the streak unit and messages, the streak intro `#streak-intro`, the legend `#legend-hit` / `#legend-flag`, the footer `#footer-guide`, and FACT_1 (overridden by `guide.copy.fact` when set).
- Not changed: the wordmark and the bib sponsor label "Weekly 150", the sample seed, Undo, the plan card, the timer (it had no target copy), theme, and tokens.

**F1, versions never repeat (ABA after Clear all).** Before, `user_data.version` started at 1 on insert and counted up by one, so a row deleted by Clear all and inserted again came back at version 1 and a device still holding version 1 kept its stale cache on refresh and overwrote the new row without a conflict. Versions have gaps and are only compared for equality; the store needed no change. Applied with `npm run db:reset` (local test accounts wiped).
- pgTAP (`supabase/tests/accounts.test.sql`, 71): literal versions replaced by relational checks (captured with `set_config('t.*')`, new version greater than the old, stale version conflicts); new checks for the sequence, the bigint column, sequence privileges, and insert, delete, insert again, then a put with the old version gives `ok = false` and leaves the new row.
