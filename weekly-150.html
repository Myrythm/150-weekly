        Why bother, briefly.

        You already know exercise is good for you. Here is how little it takes to matter.

          Weekly 150 follows the WHO guideline for adults: at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, or 75 of vigorous, plus muscle work on 2 or more days. Read the WHO guideline (opens in a new tab)

          Your log lives in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

          General information, not medical advice. If you have a heart condition or other health concern, check with a clinician before starting vigorous exercise.
